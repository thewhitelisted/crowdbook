crowdbook 0.13.0 is the first release that installs with pip, and the first that fits itself to a
recorded market and says whether the fit can be trusted for testing strategies.

## Install

```bash
pip install crowdbook-engine==0.13.0 crowdbook-analysis==0.13.0 \
    --find-links https://github.com/thewhitelisted/crowdbook/releases/expanded_assets/v0.13.0
```

Wheels for Linux (x86-64 and arm64, manylinux) and macOS (Apple silicon, 14 or later), for
Python 3.12 and every later version (not the free-threaded builds, which build from source). `crowdbook-engine` holds the `crowdbook` command and the
learning pool `crowdbook_learn`; `crowdbook-analysis` the commands that fit and check markets;
`crowdbook`, the client for served markets, is attached too. Start with
[docs/quickstart.md](https://github.com/thewhitelisted/crowdbook/blob/v0.13.0/docs/quickstart.md)
and [docs/tutorial.md](https://github.com/thewhitelisted/crowdbook/blob/v0.13.0/docs/tutorial.md),
from a recording to a tested strategy in about fifteen minutes.

## New

**Fitting a market to a recording, and checking the fit**
([docs/validation.md](https://github.com/thewhitelisted/crowdbook/blob/v0.13.0/docs/validation.md)):
- *Tapes*, one format for recorded and simulated markets: book snapshots, trades with their
  aggressor, a reference value, scores for games, labels; CSV or Parquet. `same_order` in
  `tape.toml` says that a venue stamps each fill of one order apart. `crowdbook-tape` turns a
  recording in plain CSV, quotes and trades with their times, into a tape, cut into markets of a
  set length.
- `crowdbook-validate`: about 50 statistics of the book, trades, returns, order flow, what resting
  orders and standing quotes earn, and how fast the price follows the value, each with an
  interval from resampling markets, a distance, and pass or fail; charts with `--plots`.
- `crowdbook-calibrate`: any scenario with `${placeholders}` fitted by simulated moments and
  CMA-ES, judged on held-out seeds against what chance gives, with 68% intervals (`--refits` to
  count the search's own noise), recovery of known settings with `--truth`, twins for markets
  that differ such as games, `--start` to carry a fit on, `around`, `window` and `phase` to fit
  part of each market.
- `crowdbook-sweep`, the no-false-edge check: generic makers, trend followers, reversal traders
  and flow followers run alone in a fitted market, each family's best judged on fresh markets and
  flagged if it makes money after fees.
- A learner in the check: `crowdbook-sweep` and `crowdbook-assess` also train a small strategy in
  the fitted market itself, a linear policy over signals any seat could compute, and flag it the
  same way, since a fitted market can pay what no single rule takes but a learner finds
  (`--learner-generations` and `--learner-markets` size its training, `--no-learner` leaves it
  out).
- `crowdbook-assess`: from a recording to a verdict in one command, with exit code 0 only for a
  usable fit.
- `crowdbook-draws` and `crowdbook_learn.evaluate`: a strategy that names its own prices, tested
  across draws from a fit's uncertainty, with its profit and interval, fills, queue position,
  fill rate, waits, markouts at 1, 10 and 60 s, and capacity at larger sizes.
- `crowdbook-generate`: labelled synthetic markets by the thousand, in Parquet shards.

**The learning environment** (M19): many markets stepped at once from Python, in C++ on threads
of their own, each through one seat; `Pool.step_orders` for explicit orders; settings drawn for
each episode; Gymnasium's vector API; and `Pool.copy`, a market copied mid-episode, which goes on
as its source does given the same actions, for strategies that search ahead.

**For agents driven turn by turn**: the gateway gives every seat the same bytes for the same
messages at the same wall times (tested), and `Gateway::finishesAt` says when a session will end.

**The engine**:
- Prediction markets on a game clock: scores that move the probability, pre-game drift and news,
  contracts on either side, a draw, a total or a spread, scores replayed from a recording, and a
  value path replayed from a recorded reference price.
- An exchange can hold new orders for a stretch of the run, cancels too if asked, and clear its
  book at set times.
- Market makers can quote around the true value as they see it, quote a ladder of levels, leave
  their quotes where they are until their fair price moves by `requote_threshold`, and stay away or
  requote only now and then over a stretch.
- A link's delay can be a measured distribution, given by quantiles; a learning seat's feed can
  call a decision when it moves, and can publish the true value itself.
- Noise traders' market orders can carry a worst price; an agent group may have a count of 0.
- Faster: the kernel's event queue is a radix heap; fits run about twice as fast with the same
  results.

## Changes that may need yours

- `find_package(crowdbook 0.13)`: until 1.0 only the same minor version is compatible.
- Scenario files, session files and the protocol keep their version numbers, and sessions
  recorded by earlier versions still replay.
- The analysis commands look for `crowdbook` on the path when there is no build in the checkout,
  and take a directory as `--crowdbook` for the `crowdbook` in it.
- `crowdbook-sweep` and `crowdbook-assess` need the learning module, `crowdbook_learn` (from
  `crowdbook-engine`, or a build with Python), unless given `--no-learner`; `crowdbook-assess`'s
  verdict, and its exit code, now count the learner too.
- A fit leaves out of its target any statistic that more than a tenth of the recording's resamples
  leave unmeasured, and says which.
