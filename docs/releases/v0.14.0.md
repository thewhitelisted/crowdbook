crowdbook 0.14.0 gives market makers order flow to read, and stops every market maker meeting its
own quotes.

## Install

```bash
pip install crowdbook-engine==0.14.0 crowdbook-analysis==0.14.0
```

## New

- Market makers can read order flow into their fair price and take quotes it has moved past
  ([docs/scenarios.md](https://github.com/thewhitelisted/crowdbook/blob/v0.14.0/docs/scenarios.md)):
  `flow_impact` moves the fair price by each lot of aggressive volume, `flow_memory` lets that
  impact fade, and `take_through` sends an immediate-or-cancel order when the fair price is past
  someone else's best quote by more than its threshold. Each is off by default.

## Changes that may need yours

- **Market makers behave differently by default.** When a maker's new bid would reach one of its
  own asks as they stand, the asks now move first and the bid after. In 0.13 it moved its bids
  first, and the exchange cancelled a bid that met its own ask as
  a self-trade, so a maker whose fair price jumped past its own ask lost that bid until its next
  requote. In a market whose price jumps, a maker lost 29 bids that way in 300 seconds; now it
  loses none. Markets whose prices move less than a maker's spread between requotes run exactly as
  before: the golden runs keep their hashes. Fits of markets whose prices jump, and the tutorial's
  figures, change; refit a scenario fitted with 0.13 before comparing results across the two.
